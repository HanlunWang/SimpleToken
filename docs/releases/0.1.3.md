Grok Bot and Cursor join Claude Code and Codex.

## New

- **Grok Bot and Cursor usage.** Both run through your Cursor account, so SimpleToken syncs their usage from it (through tokscale, now 4.18) once an hour, after you sign in to the Cursor desktop app on this Mac. Grok Bot appears as its own tool with its own logo, and Grok models are grouped under xAI.
- **The X Pass weekly limit.** The included-usage window that Grok Bot and Cursor share shows up as a third limit, with the plan name, the reset time, the pace marker and the projection, in the Limits widget, the drop-down panel, the menu bar and the alerts. Nothing to paste: it is read with the Cursor desktop app's sign-in.
- **Every plan in the Limits widget.** The large widget lists each provider's plan beside its logo instead of squeezing them into one field.

## Install

Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.

Download `SimpleToken-0.1.3.zip` below, unzip it and move `SimpleToken.app` to `/Applications`, replacing the older version (quit it first). Your settings and history stay as they are. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

The SHA-256 of the zip is in `SimpleToken-0.1.3.zip.sha256`.
