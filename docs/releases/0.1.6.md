Limit forecasts that keep up with how hard you are working.

## Changed

- **Forecasts follow your pace.** Once 5% of a limit window has passed, the forecast on each dial is where the window ends up if you keep going as you have since it opened. 0.1.5 assumed the rest of the window would look like an average day of your past two weeks, which read far too low after a heavy start. In a window's first minutes or hours, too early for the pace to mean much, Claude windows still go by your usual hours.
- **Forecasts past the limit say so.** A window heading over its limit now shows the full number (for example "→ 142%") instead of stopping at 100%.

## Install

Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.

Download `SimpleToken-0.1.6.zip` below, unzip it and move `SimpleToken.app` to `/Applications`, replacing the older version (quit it first). Your settings and history stay as they are. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

The SHA-256 of the zip is in `SimpleToken-0.1.6.zip.sha256`.
